## 📄 Executive Statement & Pedagogical Scope

This repository presents the **6-Vector Ontological Framework**, a theoretical and mathematical reframing of continuous-medium physics designed for research and learning institutions. 

**Our position is direct, precise, and empirical:**
* **We do not reject established physics.** The empirical telemetry, astronomical observations, and experimental data accumulated by modern physics are valid and accurate.
* **We adjust the spatial baseline.** Conventional models treat space as an empty geometric void and rely on isolated vector fields, dark matter placeholders, or unmodeled drift corrections to reconcile discrepancies.
* **We model what works mathematically at scale.** By modeling space as a dense, continuous physical medium, magnetic field vectors and bulk velocity vectors naturally integrate into a unified six-dimensional tensor field $\Psi(6v)$. Observed spatial anomalies resolve natively as dynamic substrate flow without altering physical constants or inventing unobserved mass.

## 🏛️ Epistemological & Philosophical Foundations

Classical and modern physics frequently separate geometric space from the physical fields existing within it. The 6-Vector Framework restores **Continuum Mechanics** to spatial geometry through three core pillars:

1. **Substrate Primacy:** Space is not an empty vacuum; it is modeled as a continuous, dynamic physical medium. Geometry is an emergent property of substrate energy density and localized flow dynamics.
2. **Unified Tensor State Equation:** Rather than evaluating kinetic movement and electromagnetic forces through isolated vectors, physical interactions are resolved within a unified state tensor $\Psi(6v)$.
3. **Native Anomaly Resolution:** Observed discrepancies—such as satellite flyby velocity anomalies, rotational velocity plateaus, and localized directional drift—resolve natively as hydrodynamic flow dynamics without invoking unobserved matter or modifying physical constants.

### Hydrodynamic Field Coupling & Continuity Equations

The evolution of localized field dynamics and pressure distribution within the dense continuous medium is governed by the hydrodynamic continuum formulation:

$$\frac{\partial \Psi(6v)}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{V} \cdot \nabla)\Psi(6v) = -\frac{1}{\rho_s}\nabla P_s + \nu_s \nabla^2 \Psi(6v) + \mathbf{\mathcal{F}}_{\text{mag}}$$

Where:
* $\rho_s$ is the continuous substrate density parameter.
* $P_s$ is the localized spatial scalar pressure potential.
* $\nu_s$ represents the kinematic viscosity coefficient of the continuous substrate.
* $\mathbf{\mathcal{F}}_{\text{mag}}$ models field vector coupling across localized rotational components.
