## 📄 Executive Statement & Pedagogical Scope

This repository presents the **6-Vector Ontological Framework**, a theoretical and mathematical reframing of continuous-medium physics designed for research and learning institutions. 

**Our position is direct, precise, and empirical:**
* **We do not reject established physics.** The empirical telemetry, astronomical observations, and experimental data accumulated by modern physics are valid and accurate.
* **We adjust the spatial baseline.** Conventional models treat space as an empty geometric void and rely on isolated vector fields, dark matter placeholders, or unmodeled drift corrections to reconcile discrepancies.
* **We model what works mathematically at scale.** By modeling space as a dense, continuous physical medium, magnetic field vectors and bulk velocity vectors naturally integrate into a unified six-dimensional tensor field $\Psi(6v)$. Observed spatial anomalies resolve natively as dynamic substrate flow without altering physical constants or inventing unobserved mass.
